Common questions

What is final expense insurance?

It is a small whole life insurance policy, often $5,000 to $25,000, meant to help pay for a funeral, burial or cremation, and other final bills.

Do I need a medical exam?

Many final expense plans don't require an exam. You answer health questions by phone. Some plans accept people with health problems, though they may have a waiting period.

Can I buy a policy for my parent?

Yes, with your parent's permission. They must agree to the policy and usually take part in the phone call.

How much does it cost?

Price depends on age, health, state and coverage amount. A licensed agent can give you exact quotes on the call.
